About Us

Driving evidence. Enabling change.

Synergetic Insights and Consulting is a Jordan-based consulting company dedicated to advancing evidence-driven decision-making and organizational effectiveness across development, humanitarian, and private sectors. We specialize in Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL), Collaborating, Learning, and Adapting (CLA), data collection and analysis, capacity building, and institutional development.

Our approach is rooted in partnership: we work with and alongside our clients to generate insights that are practical, strategic, and grounded in context. Whether conducting evaluations, building MEL systems, or facilitating learning agendas, Synergetic empowers organizations to amplify their impact and foster sustainable change.

Our Vision

The region's most trusted MEL partner

Our vision is to be the region's most trusted partner for organizations striving to turn evidence into action — providing insights that spark meaningful decisions and drive transformative, sustainable impact.

Our Mission

Evidence at every stage

Our mission is to deliver consulting services of the highest quality by integrating research, evaluation, and adaptive learning into every stage of programming, enabling our clients to achieve results that matter and create lasting change.
