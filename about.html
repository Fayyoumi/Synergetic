About Us

Who We Are

A Jordan-based consulting firm dedicated to helping organizations generate credible evidence and act on it.

Our Mission

Turning evidence into better decisions

Synergetic Insights and Consulting works with donors, NGOs, government institutions, and private-sector clients across Jordan and the region to design, implement, and analyze Monitoring, Evaluation, Learning (MEL), Knowledge Management, Research, and Data Analysis work.

We believe good decisions start with good evidence — collected rigorously, analyzed clearly, and communicated in a way that people can actually use.

Our Values

What guides our work

1

Rigor

We apply sound methodologies and quality assurance at every stage of our work.

2

Integrity

We report findings honestly, independent of what a client may want to hear.

3

Local Context

Our work is grounded in a deep understanding of Jordan and the region.

4

Utilization Focus

We design every product — from indicators to reports — to be genuinely usable.

5

Partnership

We work closely with client teams so that learning is shared, not just delivered.

6

Confidentiality

We handle client and respondent data with strict confidentiality and care.
