Our Services

What We Offer

End-to-end support across Monitoring, Evaluation, Learning, Knowledge Management, Research, and Data Analysis.

01

Monitoring & Evaluation

We design and implement M&E systems and evaluations that track progress and measure results against your program's theory of change.

  • Baseline, midline & endline surveys
  • Performance monitoring & indicator tracking systems
  • Formative, summative & impact evaluations
  • Theory of change & logframe development
  • Real-time monitoring & field data collection
  • Third-party monitoring
02

Learning & Knowledge Management

We help teams capture, share, and use what they learn so that programs continuously improve.

  • After-action reviews & lessons-learned exercises
  • Learning agendas & adaptive management support
  • Knowledge products: briefs, case studies, toolkits
  • Capacity building & MEL training workshops
  • Facilitation of learning & reflection sessions
  • Documentation of best practices
03

Research

We design and conduct rigorous research that answers the questions that matter most to your organization.

  • Qualitative & quantitative research design
  • Needs assessments & situation analyses
  • Feasibility & policy studies
  • Household & beneficiary surveys
  • Key informant interviews & focus group discussions
  • Literature & secondary data reviews
04

Data Analysis

We turn raw data into clear, decision-ready insight through robust analysis and visualization.

  • Quantitative & statistical analysis
  • Qualitative data analysis & coding
  • Data cleaning, management & quality assurance
  • Dashboards & data visualization
  • Survey design & digital data collection tools
  • Report writing & presentation of findings
